Research

My research interests lie in the theoretical foundations of deep learning. I seek to develop a scientific understanding of the phenomena observed in practice. My current work focuses on the theory of large language model (LLM) pretraining. Specifically, I study how optimizers, architectures, and hyperparameters affect training dynamics, data efficiency, and training-time efficiency at scale. I am also interested in neural network expressivity and the theory of operator learning.
